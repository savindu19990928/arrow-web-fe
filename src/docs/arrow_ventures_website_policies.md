## 3.9 Electric bicycle safety and responsible use

Electric bicycles are vehicles with mechanical and electrical systems. Safe use, inspection, charging, and maintenance are important.

Customers are responsible for:

- Reading and following the manufacturer's user manual, warnings, and maintenance instructions.
- Using the bicycle only for its intended purpose and within the stated load, terrain, and operating limits.
- Checking brakes, tyres, lights, steering, fasteners, and other safety-critical components before riding.
- Wearing appropriate protective equipment, including a suitable helmet.
- Following applicable road traffic, vehicle, helmet, age, and other safety requirements in the place where the bicycle is used.
- Ensuring that the bicycle is correctly assembled and adjusted before riding. Where the customer is not competent to assemble or inspect it, professional assistance should be obtained.
- Using only compatible chargers, batteries, and components approved or specified for the relevant model.

Do not ride an electric bicycle if you suspect a fault affecting the brakes, frame, steering, wheels, motor, battery, charger, or electrical system. Arrange an inspection or repair before using it again.

## 3.10 Battery and charging safety

Lithium-ion batteries require appropriate handling and care. Customers must follow the instructions supplied with the battery and bicycle.

In particular:

- Use only the charger specified or approved for the battery.
- Do not use a battery or charger that is visibly damaged, swollen, leaking, unusually hot, or producing an unusual smell.
- Do not open, puncture, crush, modify, or attempt an unauthorised repair of a battery.
- Keep batteries and chargers away from excessive heat, open flames, and conditions prohibited by the manufacturer's instructions.
- Charge and store batteries in accordance with the manufacturer's guidance.
- Keep batteries away from children and protect terminals from short circuits.
- Do not dispose of batteries in ordinary household waste where separate battery disposal is required.

If a battery shows signs of damage or abnormal behaviour, stop using and charging it and seek appropriate professional advice. Contact us for product-related support. Battery transport and return arrangements may be subject to special requirements.
